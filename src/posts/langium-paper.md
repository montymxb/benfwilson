Our [Langium paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3837062.3838881) was accepted and published as part of the [MODELS Companion '26 conference](https://conf.researchr.org/details/models-2026/models-2026-tools-and-demonstrations/23/Langium-A-TypeScript-Based-Language-Workbench-for-Domain-Specific-Languages) this year.

If you're not familiar with [Langium](https://langium.org/), it's a TypeScript-based language engineering framework (or language workbench). [TypeFox](https://www.typefox.io/), where I work, is well known for it, and it makes building rich language support for custom languages quite easy.

I'm _biased_ of course, but I still think it's pretty great tooling.

With the wide adoption of Langium we've been seeing, most of the TypeFox team wanted to get a citable paper out into the wild.
Until now, other papers _attempting_ to cite a core Langium source often had to settle for the website.
Going forward, this should be much better for anyone wishing to reference our work.
