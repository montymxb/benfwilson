We recently got our [Langium paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3837062.3838881) accepted and published as part of the [MODELS Companion '26 conference](https://conf.researchr.org/details/models-2026/models-2026-tools-and-demonstrations/23/Langium-A-TypeScript-Based-Language-Workbench-for-Domain-Specific-Languages) this year.

If you're not familiar with [Langium](https://langium.org/), it's a TS-based language engineering framework (or language workbench). It's something that TypeFox (where I work) is well-known for, and makes it quite easy to build rich language support for custom languages.

I'm _biased_ of course, but it's still pretty great tooling in my own opinion.

Really great stuff, especially due to the wide adoption of Langium that we've been seeing overall.
It was coming to a point where most of the TypeFox team wanted to get a citable paper out into the wild.
Previously there were other papers _attempting_ to cite a core Langium source, which was often-times just the website.
Going forward this should be much better for those wishing to reference the project.
